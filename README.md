# TCG Unboxing Sim

A free rock and mineral card collection game. Open nine editions of illustrated rock packs, discover 240 cards based on 48 rocks and minerals, and organize your finds in the vault. Each pack edition features a signature rock. Open one free pack at a time with one of three odds presets. Owned rocks earn small amounts of virtual money when tapped and while away, and a sale credits the amount the rock set aside.

## Play or install

Play at [tcgunboxingsim.com](https://tcgunboxingsim.com/). On iPhone, open the game in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if shown. On Android, use Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu.

Progress saves on the current device. Sign in to sync across devices if cloud saves are configured. Existing saves keep their former cards in an archive, outside the playable rock vault.

## Develop locally

Serve the project with `python -m http.server` and open `http://localhost:8000`. See [SUPABASE_SETUP.md](SUPABASE_SETUP.md) to configure optional account and cloud save support.
