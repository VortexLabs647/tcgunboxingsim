# TCG Unboxing Sim

A free rock and mineral card collection game. Open 16 editions of illustrated rock packs, discover 240 cards based on 48 rocks and minerals, and organize your finds in the vault. Each pack edition features a signature rock. Buy one pack at a time using virtual money and one of two odds presets. Owned rocks earn small amounts while away, and every newly pulled card can be sold immediately. A Common pull unlocks Rock Match. Complete it to play Crystal Drop, a peg puzzle that tracks your best score. Rock Arena has 12 campaign levels with completion and first-clear rewards, plus quick AI battles and private rooms.

## Play or install

Play at [tcgunboxingsim.com](https://tcgunboxingsim.com/). On iPhone, open the game in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if shown. On Android, use Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu.

Progress saves on the current device. Sign in to sync across devices if cloud saves are configured. Existing saves keep their former cards in an archive, outside the playable rock vault.

## Develop locally

Serve the project with `python -m http.server` and open `http://localhost:8000`. See [SUPABASE_SETUP.md](SUPABASE_SETUP.md) to configure optional account and cloud save support.
